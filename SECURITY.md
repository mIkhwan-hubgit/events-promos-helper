# Security policy

Thanks for helping keep What's On Malaysia and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private reporting instead:
the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**. I'll reply within 7 days.

## In scope

- The live site (https://ikhwan-events-helper.vercel.app) and its API
- Sign-in, accounts and plans (for example, seeing or changing someone else's plans)
- The code in this repository, including the daily refresh job

## Out of scope

- The third-party websites that events are collected from
- Denial-of-service against the free hosting plans
